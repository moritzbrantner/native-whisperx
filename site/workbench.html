Why the split matters

          

            native-whisperx owns workflow composition and product behavior. Reusable ASR, alignment, diarization,
            and model-runtime mechanics stay in their lower-level packages. The Pages app therefore demonstrates
            browser-safe capabilities and composes native behavior without creating a second implementation of the engine.
